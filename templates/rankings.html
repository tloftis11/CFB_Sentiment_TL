How scores are calculated
Each team gets a Quality score and a Sentiment score (both 0–100). Divergence is the gap between where a team ranks in public attention vs. where it ranks in on-field quality.
Quality Score (0–100)
SP+ Rating 100% — only input
SP+ is ESPN's predictive efficiency metric. Higher = objectively better team. Normalised across all 138 FBS teams so the best team scores 100 and the worst scores 0.
Sentiment Score (0–100)
AP Top 25 teams
AP Poll rank 40%
Google Trends 35%
Recruiting rank 25%
Unranked teams
Google Trends 58%
Recruiting rank 42%
Unranked teams skip the AP component so the two groups share the same 0–100 scale. AP is normalised within the 25-team pool (rank 1 = 100, rank 25 = 0).
Divergence Score
Formula Sentiment %ile − Quality %ile
Both scores are converted to percentile ranks across all 138 FBS teams before subtracting. A score of +30 means a team's sentiment rank is 30 percentile points above its quality rank.
